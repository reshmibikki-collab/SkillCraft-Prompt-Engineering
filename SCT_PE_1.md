**Prompt 1 – Vague Prompt**

“Explain cloud computing.”

Cloud computing is the delivery of computing services such as storage, servers, databases, and software over the internet instead of using only a local computer.

**Main Features of Cloud Computing:**

&#x20;  Storage: Stores files and data online.

&#x20;  Accessibility: Allows users to access data from different devices.

&#x20;  Scalability: Resources can be increased or decreased when needed.

&#x20;  Flexibility: Services can be used from almost anywhere with an internet connection.

**Types of Cloud Computing**

**1.Public Cloud:**      

&#x20;      

&#x20;       Services are provided over the internet.

&#x20;      

&#x20;       Examples: Online storage and web-based applications.



**2.Private Cloud:**

&#x20;          

&#x20;              Used by a single organization.

&#x20;         

&#x20;              Provided greater control over data and resources.



**3.Hybrid Cloud:**



&#x20;               Combines public and private cloud services.

&#x20;             

&#x20;                Provides flexibility for organizations.

**Applications of Cloud Computing** 

&#x20;  Online file storage

&#x20;  Online education

&#x20;  Business applications.

&#x20;  Video streaming.

&#x20;  Data backup.

**Prompt 1 – Improved Prompt**

“Explain cloud computing in 5 simple bullet points for a college student. Include its meaning, three main types, three benefits, and two real-life examples. Use easy-to-understand language and keep the explanation concise.”

&#x20; Meaning: Cloud computing provides computing services such as storage and software through the internet. 

&#x20;Types: The three main types are public cloud, private cloud, and hybrid cloud. 

&#x20;Benefits: It provides flexibility, reduces hardware costs, and allows users to access resources from anywhere. 

&#x20; Example 1: Google Drive allows users to store and access files online. 

&#x20;Example 2: Online learning platforms use cloud services to provide educational content to students.

**Comparison \& Analysis**

**Comparison:**

The response to the vague prompt was more general and broad, covering the meaning, features, types, and applications of cloud computing.

The improved prompt produced a more focused and organized response. It followed specific instructions by providing 5 key points, 3 main types, 3 benefits, and 2 real-life examples. The language was simple and appropriate for a college student.

**Analysis:**

Adding specific instructions about the audience, content, structure, and language made the AI response more focused, relevant, and useful. The improved prompt reduced ambiguity and helped generate a clear and well-organized response.



**Prompt 2: Vague Prompt**

“Explain time management.”

Time management is the process of planning and organizing how to use your time effectively. It helps people complete their tasks on time and reduce stress.

Some important time management methods include creating a schedule, setting priorities, avoiding distractions, and taking regular breaks. Good time management can improve productivity and help maintain a balance between study, work, and personal activities.

**Prompt 2 – Improved Prompt**

“Explain time management in 5 simple points for a college student. Include its importance, 3 effective techniques, and 2 real-life examples. Use simple language and give a short conclusion.”

&#x20; Meaning: Time management means planning and organizing your time to complete tasks efficiently. 

&#x20; Importance: It helps college students complete assignments on time and reduce stress. 

&#x20; Technique 1: Create a daily schedule and list tasks according to priority. 

&#x20; Technique 2: Avoid distractions such as unnecessary social media use while studying. 

&#x20; Technique 3: Use short breaks between study sessions to stay focused.

Real-life Examples:

A student creates a study timetable before examinations. 

A student divides time between classes, assignments, and personal activities. 

Conclusion: Good time management helps students become more organized, productive, and confident.

**Comparison \& Analysis**

**Comparison:**

The response to the vague prompt was more general, covering the meaning, methods, benefits, and importance of time management.

The improved prompt produced a more focused and organized response. It followed specific instructions by providing 5 key points, 3 techniques, 2 real-life examples, and a conclusion. The language was simple and appropriate for a college student.

**Analysis:**

Adding specific instructions about the audience, structure, content, and length made the AI response more focused, organized, and useful.



**Prompt 3 – Vague Prompt**

“Explain internet safety.”

Internet safety means protecting yourself and your personal information while using the internet. It involves using strong passwords, avoiding suspicious links, protecting personal information, and being careful when communicating with strangers online.

Internet safety is important because cybercriminals can use the internet to steal information, spread malware, or trick users through scams and phishing messages.





**Prompt 3 – Improved Prompt**

“Explain internet safety in 5 simple points for a college student. Include common online risks, 3 safety practices, and 2 real-life examples. Use easy language and provide a short conclusion.”



&#x20; Meaning: Internet safety means protecting your personal information and devices while using the internet. 

&#x20; Online Risk – Phishing: Fake emails or messages may try to steal passwords or personal information. 

&#x20; Online Risk – Malware: Harmful software can damage devices or steal information. 

&#x20; Safety Practices: Use strong passwords and enable two-factor authentication. 

&#x20; Safety Practices: Avoid suspicious links and never share sensitive information with unknown people. 

Real-life Examples:

A student receives a fake email asking for their college account password. 

A user receives a suspicious link claiming they have won a prize. 

**Conclusion**: Following basic internet safety practices can help protect your accounts, devices, and personal information.

**Comparison \& Analysis**

**Comparison:**

The response to the vague prompt was broad, covering the meaning of internet safety, common risks, and general safety measures.

The improved prompt produced a more focused and organized response. It followed specific instructions by providing 5 key points, common online risks, 3 safety practices, 2 real-life examples, and a conclusion. The language was simple and suitable for a college student.

**Analysis:**

Adding specific instructions about the audience, content, structure, and language made the AI response more relevant, clear, and useful. It also reduced ambiguity and helped the AI provide the information in the required format.
