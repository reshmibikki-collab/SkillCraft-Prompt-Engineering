**TASK 02 — PROMPTING FOR CREATIVITY**

**Variant 2 – Youthful Few-Shot Prompt:**

Create an advertisement for a smart study app. Use the following examples to guide the style and tone:

Example 1:

“Less stress. More progress. Make every study session count.”

Example 2:

“Plan it. Learn it. Ace it. Your smarter study journey starts here.”

Now write a new advertisement for the smart study app. Make it catchy, positive, youthful, and suitable for college students. Include a short slogan and a clear call to action. Keep it under 100 words.

**Variant 3 – Premium Few-Shot Prompt:**

Create an advertisement for a smart study app in a premium, emotional, and elegant brand style.

Example 1:

“Your goals deserve more than a plan. They deserve a smarter way forward.”

Example 2:

“Designed for focused minds. Created for meaningful progress.”

Now write a new advertisement for the smart study app. Target ambitious and environmentally conscious young adults. Make the tone sophisticated, emotional, and inspiring. Include a memorable headline, a short body, and a strong call to action. Keep it under 100 words.

Every goal begins with a moment of focus.

**Comparison \& Analysis:**

**Variant 1 – Basic Prompt:**

The basic prompt produced a straightforward advertisement that focused on the app's main features, such as study planning, reminders, note organization, and progress tracking.

Variant 2 – Youthful Few-Shot Prompt:

The few-shot prompt produced a more catchy and engaging advertisement. The examples helped guide the writing style, while the college-student audience made the content more youthful, relatable, and motivating.

Variant 3 – Premium Few-Shot Prompt:

The premium few-shot prompt produced a sophisticated and emotional advertisement. It used elegant language and focused more on ambition, personal growth, purpose, and achieving meaningful goals.

**Overall Analysis:**

The results show that prompt structure, examples, tone, and audience instructions influence the AI's creative output. The basic prompt generated a general advertisement, while the few-shot prompts created more distinctive styles. The examples helped guide the model toward the desired tone and persona.
